# Data Science From Scratch

## Chapter-1: Introduction

### 🌍 1. Data is everywhere
 
Today, everything around us is creating data:
 
- Websites record every click we make.
- Our mobile phone keeps track of where we are, every second.
- Fitness bands record heart rate, sleep, steps and food habits.
- Smart cars, smart homes and online shops are all collecting our habits.
- The Internet itself is like one huge library of facts movies, sports, music, government numbers and much more.


#### 📌 What is Data?

**Data** means raw facts and figures that are collected and stored, like numbers, words, images, clicks or measurements. On its own, data doesn't mean much. It becomes useful **information** only after we organise and analyse it.

#### Simple examples
- Marks of students in an exam: `85, 72, 90`
- Names of customers in a shop
- Your phone's location, recorded every second
- The pages you click on a website
- Temperature readings from a weather station

#### Data vs Information
| Data | Information |
|---|---|
| Raw, unorganised facts | Processed, meaningful facts |
| `48000, 61500, 79167` | "Data scientists with 5+ years of experience earn about 65% more than freshers" |

### 🧑‍💻 2. What is Data Science?
 
 **A data scientist is a person who takes messy data and gets useful insights from it.**

#### Real-life examples given in the book
 
| Company / Group | What they did with data |
|---|---|
| **OkCupid** (dating site) | Studied users' answers to find which simple questions predict dating behaviour. |
| **Facebook** | Used home town and current location to study migration patterns and where fans of different football teams live. |
| **Target** (US retail shop) | Used shopping history to guess which customers were pregnant, so it could show them baby products. |
| **Obama campaign (2012)** | Used data to find which voters needed attention, how to ask for donations, and where to focus "go and vote" efforts. |
 
Data science is also used for good things, like making governments work better, helping homeless people and improving public health.
